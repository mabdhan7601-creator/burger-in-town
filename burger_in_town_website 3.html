Our Story

Born From a Passion
For Real Burgers

Launched in March 2023, Burger In Town was built on one simple belief — Islamabad deserved a smash burger joint that didn't cut corners. Owner Asad personally oversees every patty, every sauce, every bite.

Fresh beef, in-house sauces pressed on a blazing griddle. No freezer. No shortcuts. Just pure burger satisfaction.

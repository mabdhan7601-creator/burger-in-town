Our Story

Born From a Passion
For Real Burgers

Launched in March 2023, Burger In Town was built on one simple belief — Islamabad deserved a smash burger joint that didn't cut corners. Owner Asad personally oversees every patty, every sauce, every bite that leaves the kitchen.

We source fresh beef, craft signature sauces in-house, and press every patty on a blazing hot griddle to get that perfect crust. No freezer. No shortcuts. Just pure burger satisfaction.
